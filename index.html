Frequently Asked Questions

How much detail do you need to write my song?

The more the better, but be fairly specific about the things you care most about.

How long does the process take?

We turn most songs around within 2 days of getting lyrics approved by you.

What if I don't like the song for whatever reason?

Let us know, provide feedback and direction and we'll keep working to get it just right. Honestly this has only happened one time, but after a revision of the genre the customer loved the new version.

What streaming services does the song get uploaded to?

It would be a shorter list to say where it won't end up. It's basically all of them.

How long till the song is up on the streaming platforms?

Varies depending on how busy those platforms are, but most are done within a week, two weeks at most.
